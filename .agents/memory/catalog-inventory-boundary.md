---
name: Catalog and inventory boundary
description: Which shop materials are sellable versus internal operating supplies.
---

Materials that customers can buy or that are directly part of a sellable service belong in the active Catalog. This includes paper, laminating materials, tape, adhesive tape, stationery, and finishing supplies. Machine-only consumables such as photocopier ink remain internal and are not sold through the Catalog.

**Why:** The owner sells or charges for these materials/services at the counter, while items such as ink are consumed by the machine and should not appear as customer-facing products.

**How to apply:** Keep a product active and priced per the customer-facing unit when it is sellable. Keep only genuinely non-sellable machine supplies in the internal classification; preserve any separate on-hand stock records without treating them as customer products.
